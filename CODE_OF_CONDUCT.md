# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming, curious, and safe for everyone.

Contributors must communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback, protect other people's privacy, and keep discussion focused on the project. Harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct directed at people, threats, doxxing, trolling, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/sanay-ourora/boobs-pictures-app/security/advisories/new). Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as possible.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
